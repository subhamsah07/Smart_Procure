# SmartProcure

### Smart Digital Procurement Platform for Farmers

SmartProcure is a farmer-first digital platform designed to simplify the agricultural procurement process by reducing long waiting times, improving queue visibility, and providing farmers with a structured and transparent procurement journey.

The platform enables farmers to discover procurement centres, book procurement slots, receive tokens, track their queue status, and monitor their procurement and payment status through a unified platform.

---

## 🚨 Problem

During peak procurement seasons, farmers may have to spend several hours or even days waiting at procurement centres.

Common challenges include:

- Long and unpredictable queues
- Lack of information about waiting time
- Difficulty planning when to reach the procurement centre
- Limited visibility into procurement status
- Digital accessibility barriers for rural farmers
- Dependence on manual processes and intermediaries

SmartProcure aims to address these challenges through digital booking, queue management, real-time status tracking, and accessible communication channels.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

SmartProcure introduces a digital workflow that allows farmers to:

1. Register and verify their account
2. Select their crop and procurement requirements
3. Find a suitable procurement centre
4. Book a procurement appointment
5. Receive a digital token and QR code
6. Track their queue position
7. View estimated waiting time
8. Receive important notifications
9. Track procurement progress
10. Check payment status

### Core Concept

> **Book → Track → Arrive at the Right Time → Procure → Track Payment**

---

## ⭐ Key Features

### 👨‍🌾 Farmer Platform

- Secure farmer registration and authentication
- Email OTP verification
- Farmer profile management
- Crop information
- Procurement-centre discovery
- Appointment booking
- Automatic token generation
- QR-based booking verification
- Queue position tracking
- Estimated waiting-time display
- Procurement status tracking
- Payment status tracking

### 📍 Procurement Centre Management

- State-wise procurement centres
- Centre information and operating hours
- Centre availability/status
- Centre location on Google Maps
- Distance and navigation support
- Centre-specific queue information

### 📞 IVR-Based Access

To support farmers who may have difficulty using digital platforms, SmartProcure is designed to provide access through a multilingual IVR system.

Farmers can use a phone call to:

- Check procurement status
- Check booking details
- Track token/queue status
- Access important information
